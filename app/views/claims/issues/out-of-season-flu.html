What this means

NHSBSA does not accept claims for flu vaccinations given outside the dates of the seasonal vaccination programme.

If a mistake was made when recording the date the vaccination was given, you can delete the record and create a new record.

For more information, contact NHSBSA - MYS (opens in new tab).
