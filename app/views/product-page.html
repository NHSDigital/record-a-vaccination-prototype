Record a vaccination (RAVS) lets you record NHS vaccinations, including COVID-19, flu, MenB, RSV and pertussis.

Getting started

GPs

GPs can use the service for COVID-19 and flu outreach vaccinations. If you want to sign up or have questions, contact our onboarding team.

Pharmacies

Pharmacies can use the service for all NHS vaccinations offered as part of advanced services. Sign up for your pharmacy.

How it works

Record vaccinations easily

The recording journey includes shortcuts for co-administration and multiple vaccinations.

Track performance

See how many vaccines you have given and download the data you need for reporting.

Get paid automatically

Pharmacies and GPs are paid automatically for flu and COVID-19 vaccinations by the NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA), through Manage Your Service (MYS).
