Dear Example User,

You’ve been invited to use the NHS Record a vaccination service (RAVS) at Example Pharmacy.

There are 2 steps when you log in for the first time.

1. Activate your account

To log in to RAVS securely, all users can use the Okta sign-on service. We have therefore created an Okta account for you.

You’ll receive a 'Welcome to Okta' email (from noreply@okta.com).

Activate the link within 7 days.

If you cannot find the email, check your spam or junk.

2. Log in to Record a vaccination

Go to NHS Record a vaccination and select 'Log in'. You will then be asked how you want to log in.

All users can log in with Okta using their Okta username and password.

Pharmacy users can also log in with their pharmacy IT system, if it is listd on the Log in page.

Training and support

To help you get started, you can:

  • check our user guide
  • watch our training video
  • join a drop-in session
